Breathwork made simple

Guided breathing exercises to calm anxiety, improve focus, and help you sleep.

Calma is a breath coach for your pocket. Whether you're new to breathwork or already meditate regularly, Calma pairs guided breathing exercises with immersive visuals and sound to help you relax, refocus, and reset.

How it works

01

Choose a session

Pick from quick resets for anxiety, deeper relaxation routines, or focus rhythms based on how you feel.

02

Follow the pacer

A gentle, continuous waveform guides your lung expansion without jarring transitions or complicated counts.

03

Tune the sound & pace

Guided tones signal each inhale and exhale so you can close your eyes. Adjust timing down to the second whenever you want.

Designed to help you relax

Take a look inside the app. Every screen is thoughtfully created to reduce stress, calm your mind, and make breathing easy.
