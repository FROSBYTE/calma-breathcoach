Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By using the App, you consent to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

## 1. Information We Collect
Calma is designed with privacy in mind. We do not collect, store, or process any personal data.

### No Personal Data Collected
You are not required to create an account or provide any personal information to use the App. We do not collect or store your name, email address, phone number, or any other personally identifiable information.

### Local Storage
Any data stored on your device (session history, preferences) remains on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

### Usage Information
We collect data about how you use the app, such as the features you access, the amount of time you spend using the app, and other interactions with the app. This information helps us understand how users interact with Calma – Breath Coach and improve the user experience.

### In-App Purchases
If you purchase premium features through in-app purchases (IAP), payment processing is handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We do not collect, store, or have access to your payment card information—all payment data is processed directly by the platform you use.

Your purchase history may be retained by Apple or Google per their privacy policies. We receive only a transaction record to confirm your subscription status and grant access to premium features.
